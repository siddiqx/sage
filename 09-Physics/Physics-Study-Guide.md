## 3. High-Yield Topic Progression

### A. Classical Mechanics & Symmetries
- **Kinematics & Newton's Laws (OpenStax Vol 1, Ch 2–6):** Derivatives of position vectors ($v = dr/dt, a = dv/dt$), free-body diagrams, circular motion.
- **Work, Energy & Momentum (OpenStax Vol 1, Ch 7–9):** Line integrals of force ($W = \int F \cdot dr$), conservation of mechanical energy, impulse, center of mass.
- **Feynman Conceptual Pairings:**
  - Vol I, Ch. 1: *Atoms in Motion* (The atomic hypothesis and physical scale).
  - Vol I, Ch. 4: *Conservation of Energy* (The blocks of Dennis the Menace).
  - Vol I, Ch. 15: *Special Relativity* (Spacetime geometry and Lorentz transformations).

### B. Thermodynamics & Statistical Mechanics
- **Heat, Work & Entropy (OpenStax Vol 2, Ch 1–4):** First Law ($\Delta E_{\text{int}} = Q - W$), Carnot cycle efficiency, entropy calculation ($dS = dQ/T$).
- **Feynman Conceptual Pairing:**
  - Vol I, Ch. 46: *Ratchet and Pawl* (The Second Law of Thermodynamics, reversibility, and the arrow of time).

### C. Electromagnetism & Maxwell's Equations
- **Electrostatics & Gauss's Law (OpenStax Vol 2, Ch 5–8):** Electric flux ($\Phi_E = \oint E \cdot dA = q_{\text{enc}}/\epsilon_0$), potential difference ($V = -\int E \cdot dr$), capacitance.
- **Magnetism & Ampère's Law (OpenStax Vol 2, Ch 11–12):** Lorentz force ($F = q(E + v \times B)$), Biot-Savart law, Ampère's circuital law.
- **Induction & Maxwell's Equations (OpenStax Vol 2, Ch 13–16):** Faraday's Law ($\mathcal{E} = -d\Phi_B/dt$), displacement current, and electromagnetic wave propagation.
- **Feynman Conceptual Pairings:**
  - Vol II, Ch. 15: *The Vector Potential* (Physical reality of the Aharonov-Bohm effect).
  - Vol II, Ch. 20: *Maxwell's Equations in Free Space* (The unification of electricity, magnetism, and optics).
